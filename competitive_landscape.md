# Competitive Landscape: Focus Bear vs Other Productivity Apps

## Research summary

**Freedom** is a cross device app and website blocker (Mac, Windows, iOS, Android, Chrome), used by over 3 million people. Its core strength is syncing a single block session across every device at once, so a session started on desktop also blocks distracting apps on mobile. It's explicitly designed around not gamifying focus, no streaks, points, or leaderboards, on the belief that those mechanics create their own kind of dependency. It offers scheduling, locked mode, and focus sounds, but it is purely a blocker, it doesn't guide someone through a routine or structure their day.

**Cold Turkey** is a one time purchase blocker for Mac and Windows only (no mobile support). Its standout feature is how difficult it makes bypassing an active block once locked, reviewers consistently describe it as the hardest blocker to circumvent, with a "Frozen Turkey" mode that can lock a user out of their entire computer for a scheduled break. All data is stored locally rather than in the cloud. Like Freedom, it's a blocking tool first, it has a Pomodoro timer but no guided routines or habit structure.

**Tiimo** is a visual daily planner built specifically for ADHD and autistic users, with over 500,000 users and NHS recognition. Its core idea is representing time visually, a color coded, icon based timeline, rather than a text based to do list, since many ADHD/autistic users experience "time blindness" and struggle to feel abstract schedules. It includes gentle transition nudges, an AI co-planner for breaking down tasks, and widgets showing "now and next." It's day planning focused rather than deep project or task management, and its most complete feature set sits behind a subscription.

**Routinery** focuses specifically on building and executing step by step routines (morning, evening, custom), each step given a time allocation, with a countdown timer and voice guided (text to speech) prompts announcing each step aloud, which reviewers highlight as genuinely useful for time blindness since you don't need to keep checking your phone. It was Apple's App of the Day and has around 5 million users. Its main criticism in recent reviews is an increasingly aggressive paywall, with core customization features reportedly being moved behind a subscription over time, and some post update bugs around routines/timers.

**Focus Bear** (for comparison) combines the guided routine structure of Tiimo/Routinery with the cross device distraction blocking of Freedom/Cold Turkey in one tool, built by a founder with ADHD and autism. It walks users through morning and evening routines step by step in real time (not just a checklist), blocks distracting sites/apps across all devices during work or routine time using an AI relevance check, includes break reminders, and has a "Late No More" feature using escalating notifications to prevent lateness to meetings. It was validated in a randomized controlled trial with 446 university students showing improved outcomes.

## Reflection

**What makes Focus Bear different from these apps?**

Every other app in this comparison does one thing well: Freedom and Cold Turkey block distractions, Tiimo and Routinery guide routines. Focus Bear is the only one trying to do both in the same tool, block distractions AND guide someone through their actual routine step by step, rather than requiring someone to run two separate apps side by side. It's also explicitly designed with forgiveness in mind for missed days, which lines up with the pattern I saw in earlier research on ADHD productivity tools, that punishing inconsistency is what causes people to abandon a tool entirely.

**If I were a user, why would I choose Focus Bear over competitors?**

Mainly to avoid needing multiple apps. If I wanted routine guidance AND distraction blocking with something like Tiimo or Routinery, I'd still need a separate blocker like Freedom or Cold Turkey running alongside it. Focus Bear covers both in one place, which is less setup and fewer subscriptions to juggle. The "Late No More" escalating notification feature is also something none of the other four apps in this comparison specifically offer.

**What's one feature that other apps have that Focus Bear doesn't?**

Cold Turkey's locked mode is a genuinely harder deterrent than anything Focus Bear appears to offer, reviewers specifically describe it as nearly impossible to bypass once locked, including a full computer lockout option. Focus Bear's blocking is AI relevance based rather than a hard, near unbreakable lock, which is a reasonable design choice for a gentler experience, but it does mean a highly determined user has more ways around it than they would with Cold Turkey.

**What's one improvement Focus Bear could make?**

Routinery's voice guided (text to speech) step announcements stood out as a small but genuinely useful detail for time blindness, not needing to look at the phone to know what's next. Focus Bear's routine guidance appears to be primarily visual/on screen; adding an optional spoken step by step mode could make the routine guidance more accessible for the same reason it works well in Routinery.
