# Use of AI Tools

## Reflection:

## When should you use AI for assistance, and when should you rely on your own skills?

AI should be used as a support tool to improve productivity, generate ideas, assist with repetitive tasks, and help explain complex concepts. For example, AI can help with drafting documents, writing support code, summarising information, and identifying possible solutions.

However, I should rely on my own skills when making important decisions, analysing critical information, handling confidential data, or when professional judgment is required. AI-generated responses should always be reviewed before being used in workplace tasks.

## How can you avoid over-reliance on AI while still benefiting from it?

To avoid over-reliance on AI, I will use it as an assistant rather than a replacement for my own thinking. I will first understand the problem myself, then use AI to improve efficiency or explore additional ideas.

I will continue developing my own technical skills, problem-solving ability, and critical thinking. I will also verify AI-generated information instead of assuming it is always correct.

## What steps will you take to ensure data privacy when using AI tools?

To protect data privacy, I will:

- Avoid entering confidential company information into public AI tools.

- Never share passwords, personal information, customer data, or sensitive business documents.

- Use only approved AI tools provided by the organisation.

- Remove or anonymise sensitive information before using AI for assistance.

- Follow company policies and data protection guidelines.

After testing the AI-generated output, I reviewed the response carefully. The output was helpful, but it still required checking for accuracy, adjusting the query based on the data set structure, and ensuring the final result matched the expected business requirement.

This showed that AI can improve efficiency, but human review and understanding are still necessary.

## Best Practice I Will Follow at Focus Bear

One best practice I will follow when using AI tools at Focus Bear is:

**"Use AI responsibly by protecting confidential information and always reviewing AI-generated content before using it in professional work."**

AI will be used as a productivity tool while maintaining data privacy, accuracy, and professional judgment.
