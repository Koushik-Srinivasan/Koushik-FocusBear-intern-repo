# User Manual for Koushik

## My role
I'm a Data Analytics volunteer intern at Focus Bear. The internship runs June to December 2026, roughly 8 to 12 hours a week, Wednesday to Friday, hybrid.

## How I Work Best
I do my best work when I have a clear task broken down into steps I can tick off, and when I've had a chance to do my own research first before bringing questions to someone else. I struggle when a task is vague with no defined next step, or when I'm expected to hold a lot of context in my head instead of having it written down somewhere I can refer back to.

## Communication Style
I prefer messages to be detailed and thorough rather than short and clipped. I'd rather receive one well-explained message with the context I need than have to ask several follow-up questions to piece it together. The best way to reach me is async, in writing, so I can read it properly and respond with a considered answer rather than being put on the spot. If something is urgent, say so explicitly and tell me the deadline, that's what gets it bumped to the top of my list.

## Collaboration & Teamwork
I work best in a team when expectations and next steps are written down clearly, so everyone's working off the same information. My biggest pet peeve in teamwork is ambiguity, tasks or decisions left undefined that end up needing to be re-clarified later. If I seem quiet in a meeting, it usually means I'm processing what's been said rather than disengaged, I tend to think before I speak up.

## Focus & Productivity
I stay focused by working off structured to-do lists, breaking bigger tasks into smaller defined steps I can work through one at a time. Things that distract me are open-ended tasks without a clear scope, since I end up spending more time figuring out what to do than doing it. The best way to support my productivity is to give me clear, well-scoped tasks with enough detail that I know exactly what "done" looks like.

## Feedback & Growth
I like feedback to be direct and to the point, tell me clearly what needs to change rather than softening it so much the message gets lost. When I make a mistake, the best way to let me know is straightforwardly and early, so I can fix it before it compounds. I appreciate it when teammates give feedback tied to the specific piece of work rather than in general terms.

## Fun Facts & Quirks
One thing people often misunderstand about me is that being quiet or taking time to respond means I'm not engaged, when really I'm just working through the detail before I speak. My work superpower is turning a vague, messy problem into a structured, step-by-step plan, given my background moving from an engineering degree into data science and analytics.
